COMPLETE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT DETAIL— MATTU BRIDGE
1. Project Overview
Develop a professional, modern, responsive, secure, and user-friendly website for Mattu Bridge, a Germany-based consultancy and international services business.
Website: https://mattubridge.de
The website must present three main business categories:
1.	Educational Consultancy — Study Opportunities in Germany and Across Europe
2.	Jobs, Employment Placement & Recruitment — Job Seekers, Employers, and Job Advertisements
3.	International Events — Education, Human Rights, Youth Development, Seminars, and International Cooperation
The website must serve students, job seekers, employers, educational organizations, event participants, and potential business partners.
The design should be professional, trustworthy, accessible, and suitable for a business operating from Germany and serving people in Germany and other European countries.
Do not invent business activities, partnerships, qualifications, courses, job vacancies, events, or other information that has not been confirmed by the business owner.
